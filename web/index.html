HOW THE READING WORKS

Evidence, then language.

01

See the plate

FoodSeg103 classes and a plate ellipse establish food regions and a diner-oriented frame.

02

Place each region

Rectification, clock angle, distance from centre, and area share produce a compact spatial record.

03

Speak with care

Low-confidence regions can be hedged or omitted. The browser reads the same text shown on screen.

04

Measure with depth

Matching RGB-D imagery can estimate volume. Nutrition remains conditional on a trained or calibrated model.
